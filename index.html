About this age calculator

How is my exact age calculated?

We count whole years and months from your birth date, then the remaining days, borrowing days from the previous month the way a calendar does. Change "Age on this date" to see how old you were, or will be, on any day.

How many weeks are in a life?

A 90-year life is about 4,680 weeks. Seeing them as squares makes it easier to feel how much time you have used and how much is ahead.

Is my birth date saved or uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your date is stored only on your own device so it is ready next visit.
